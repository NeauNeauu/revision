<div class="lhead"><h2>7 · Nested formula and the chain rule</h2><span class="lpages">p. 21–24</span><button type="button" class="seen" aria-pressed="false" data-t="✓ Seen">Mark as seen</button></div>
<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p21.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p21.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>21</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p22.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p22.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>22</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p23.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p23.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>23</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p24.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p24.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>24</span></button></div>

- The network's loss is too long to expand: we differentiate it **step by step**.
- A change travels through steps: each step **multiplies** by its derivative. Toy: $2 \times (-8) = -16$.
- [[regle-chaine|Chain rule]]: $\frac{\partial c}{\partial a} = \frac{\partial c}{\partial b} \times \frac{\partial b}{\partial a}$.

<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p27.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p27.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>27</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p28.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p28.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>28</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p29.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p29.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>29</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p30.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p30.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>30</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p31.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w5-1/p31.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>31</span></button></div>

Weight $v_2$ (hidden 2 → output): $v_2 \to z \to \hat y \to L$.

1. $\partial z/\partial v_2 = h_2 = 3.5$ (the value on the same arrow).
2. $\partial \hat y/\partial z = 0.68 \times 0.32 \approx 0.22$.
3. $\partial L/\partial \hat y = 2 \times (-0.32) \approx -0.64$.

$\partial L/\partial v_2 \approx -0.64 \times 0.22 \times 3.5 \approx -0.49$, **exact**, from values the forward pass already has.
