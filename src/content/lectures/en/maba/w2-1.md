<div class="lhead"><h2>3 · The artificial neuron: M-P, Hebb, perceptron</h2><span class="lpages">p. 9–13</span><button type="button" class="seen" aria-pressed="false" data-t="✓ Seen">Mark as seen</button></div>
<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p09.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p09.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>9</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p10.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p10.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>10</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p11.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p11.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>11</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p12.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p12.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>12</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p13.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p13.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>13</span></button></div>

- [[neurone-mp]]: binary inputs, fixed weights, threshold, binary output. Weights (1, 1): threshold 2 = AND, threshold 1 = OR.
- [[hebb]]: learning = strengthening connections.
- [[perceptron]]: weights **learned** from errors.
- Limit: AND/OR are [[lineairement-separable|separable]], [[xor]] is not.

<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p16.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p16.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>16</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p17.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p17.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>17</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p18.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w2-1/p18.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>18</span></button></div>

- Basis: reverse-mode automatic differentiation (1970), applied to networks (1974), popularised in 1986.
- See [[retropropagation|backpropagation]].
