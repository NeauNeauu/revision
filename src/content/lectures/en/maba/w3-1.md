<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p03.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p03.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>3</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p04.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p04.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>4</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p05.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p05.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>5</span></button></div>

<div class="lhead"><h2>3 · One neuron = one line</h2><span class="lpages">p. 6–7</span><button type="button" class="seen" aria-pressed="false" data-t="✓ Seen">Mark as seen</button></div>
<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p06.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p06.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>6</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p07.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p07.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>7</span></button></div>

<div class="lhead"><h2>4 · From threshold to bias</h2><span class="lpages">p. 8–10</span><button type="button" class="seen" aria-pressed="false" data-t="✓ Seen">Mark as seen</button></div>
<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p08.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p08.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>8</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p09.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p09.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>9</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p10.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p10.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>10</span></button></div>

- $b = -\theta$: $w_1x_1 + w_2x_2 + b \geq 0$.
- The [[biais|bias]] is a weight on an input that is always 1 → **one update rule** for everything.
- Without a bias the line passes through the origin: you can't even build AND.

<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p11.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p11.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>11</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p12.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p12.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>12</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p13.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p13.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>13</span></button></div>

- Layer ($n$ inputs, $m$ neurons): $m \times n$ weights + $m$ biases. Network 2→2→1: **9 parameters**.

<div class="lhead"><h2>9 · Activation functions</h2><span class="lpages">p. 22–25</span><button type="button" class="seen" aria-pressed="false" data-t="✓ Seen">Mark as seen</button></div>
<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p22.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p22.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>22</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p23.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p23.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>23</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p24.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p24.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>24</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p25.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p25.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>25</span></button></div>

- The [[echelon|step]] is flat: a small change does nothing → no way to know if we improve. We need a **smooth** function.
- [[sigmoide|Sigmoid]]: output 0–1, saturates. [[tanh]]: −1 to 1, centred, saturates.
- [[relu]]: $\max(0, z)$, slope 1, no saturation, but [[dying-relu]].

<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p29.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p29.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>29</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p30.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w3-1/p30.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>30</span></button></div>
