<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p10.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p10.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>10</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p11.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p11.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>11</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p12.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p12.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>12</span></button></div>

- [[mse]] $= \frac{1}{N}\sum (y - \hat y)^2$; example: total 0.2424 / 4 = **0.0606**.
- Quantity → squared error; category → another loss.
- Data is **fixed**, only weights and biases move: $L$ is a function of the weights.

<div class="lhead"><h2>5 · A weight's slope and the update rule</h2><span class="lpages">p. 13–16</span><button type="button" class="seen" aria-pressed="false" data-t="✓ Seen">Mark as seen</button></div>
<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p13.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p13.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>13</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p14.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p14.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>14</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p15.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p15.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>15</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p16.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p16.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>16</span></button></div>

- Nudge $w$ by $+0.01$: loss $-0.0048$ → [[pente|slope]] $= -0.48$.
- Negative slope → increase $w$; positive → decrease (always **against** the slope). Steep → long step.
- Rule: $w_{new} = w_{old} - \eta \times \text{slope}$.

</section>

<div class="lslides"><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p17.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p17.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>17</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p18.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p18.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>18</span></button><button type="button" class="slide" data-src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p19.webp"><img src="/courses/maba/w4-1/p19.webp" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="675" alt=""/><span>19</span></button></div>

- A weight's effect is **proportional to its input**; input 0 → no influence.
- [[taux-apprentissage|Learning rate]] $\eta$: too small = slow, right = steady, too large = **diverges** (example: loss 16 → 31 → 61 → 120…).
