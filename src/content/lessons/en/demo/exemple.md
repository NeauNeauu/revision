## Key concepts

- Pythagorean theorem: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ in a right triangle.

## Examples

$$
3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2
$$

## Pitfalls

- $c$ is always the hypotenuse (the longest side).

## Key takeaways

- Right triangle ⇒ $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.
