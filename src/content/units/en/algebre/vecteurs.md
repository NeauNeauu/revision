## In short

A **vector** = a list of $n$ numbers (an arrow). Add them and multiply by a **scalar** entry by entry. The **dot product** gives lengths and angles.

## 1. Linear combinations

$$\mathbf v+\mathbf w=(v_1+w_1,\dots,v_n+w_n)\qquad c\mathbf v=(cv_1,\dots,cv_n)$$

A **linear combination** of $\mathbf v,\mathbf w$ is $c\mathbf v+d\mathbf w$ (with $c=d=0$: the zero vector, always included).

## 2. Dot product, length, angle

$$\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf w=\sum_i v_iw_i\qquad \|\mathbf v\|=\sqrt{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf v}\qquad \cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf w}{\|\mathbf v\|\,\|\mathbf w\|}$$

- **$\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf w=0$ ⇔ perpendicular** (Pythagoras: $\|v\|^2+\|w\|^2=\|v-w\|^2$).
- **Unit vector**: $\mathbf u=\mathbf v/\|\mathbf v\|$ (length 1). E.g. $\tfrac13(2,2,1)$.
- **Schwarz**: $|\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf w|\le\|\mathbf v\|\|\mathbf w\|$ (since $|\cos|\le1$). **Triangle**: $\|\mathbf v+\mathbf w\|\le\|\mathbf v\|+\|\mathbf w\|$.
- Handy rule: $\|\mathbf v+\mathbf w\|^2=\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf v+2\,\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf w+\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf w$.

E.g. $\mathbf v=(1,\sqrt3),\ \mathbf w=(1,0)$ → $\cos\theta=\frac{1}{2\cdot1}=\frac12$ → $\theta=60^\circ$.

## Pitfalls

- Perpendicular ⇔ dot product **zero** (not “equal to 1”).
- $\|\mathbf v\|^2$ is $\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf v$; don't forget the square root for $\|\mathbf v\|$.

## Key takeaways

- Linear combination = $c\mathbf v+d\mathbf w$; fills line → plane → space.
- $\cos\theta=\dfrac{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf w}{\|\mathbf v\|\|\mathbf w\|}$; $\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf w=0$ ⇔ ⟂.
