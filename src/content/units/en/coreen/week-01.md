## Assessment

| Item | Weight | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance | 10% | 3 tardies = 1 absence; > 30 min late = absence. More than 4 absences = fail. Each absence = −1 point on the final grade. Excused illness (certificate on the portal): twice max. |
| Assignments & participation | 30% | Submitted on time (late only with a valid reason); speaking and group participation count. |
| Midterm | 30% | Covers Weeks 2 to 7. Format (written / oral / assignment) announced in advance. |
| Final exam | 30% | Whole semester. Format announced in advance. |
